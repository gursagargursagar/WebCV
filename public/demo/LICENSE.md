# Demo assets

Everything in `public/demo/` is placeholder content that ships with Open
Portfolio so a fresh install has something to render.

**Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication).** Use, modify or
delete any of it without attribution.

## What these are

- `logos/*.svg` — wordmarks for **invented** companies (Northwind, Contoso,
  Fabrikam, Initech, Lumen Yard). These are the long-established fictional
  placeholder company names used in software documentation. They are not real
  organisations and no real trademark is reproduced here.
- `shots/*.svg` — abstract interface mockups drawn as plain shapes. They depict
  no real product.
- `avatar.svg`, `portrait.svg` — generated initials marks. **No photograph of
  any real person ships with this project.**

## Why it matters

This project is MIT-licensed and redistributed to anyone who forks it. A
licence grants rights in *our* copyright — it grants nothing in a third party's
trademark or in a client's product screenshots. Displaying a real client's logo
on your own portfolio is normally fine; shipping it inside a template that
thousands of strangers deploy is not.

**If you add demo content, it must be fictional, or yours, or CC0.** Replace
these files with your own work through the admin — do not commit a real
client's logo or artwork back to this repository.
